This policy explains how the **Hoppala: Trick Quiz** app for iOS, iPadOS and Android ("Hoppala",
"the app") handles your information. The app is developed and published by **Ulaşcan Ertürk**
("we", "us"), who is the controller of the personal data covered by this policy.

## Summary

- Hoppala has no account or sign-up; we never ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number.
- Your game progress and settings stay on your device. The built-in categories can be played
  offline.
- When new content is downloaded, our server only receives standard technical information (such as
  your IP address).
- **Answer analytics** is optional and off by default. **Challenge** requires a separate consent.
- In versions that show ads, ads are served by **Google AdMob**. Personalized ads depend on your
  consent where required.
- We do not sell your personal data. You can delete your data on our server from inside the app.

Some features (answer analytics, Challenge, ads) may not be enabled in every version of the app.
The sections below describe which data each feature processes only **when it is enabled and you
use it**.

## Information stored on your device

Hoppala stores the following **only on your device**:

- game progress, scores, best scores and the daily challenge record;
- settings: sound effects, haptics and language;
- a cache of downloaded content levels;
- your answer analytics and ad consent preferences;
- if one has been created, the installation ID and access token issued by our server (in the
  operating system's secure storage: iOS Keychain, Android Keystore-backed storage).

Because your progress is not tied to an account, it is not transferred between devices and not
backed up on our server. You can delete your progress with **Settings → Reset progress**, or delete
all data on the device by uninstalling the app.

## Information sent to our server

### Downloading content

The app downloads new categories and levels from our content server. These requests carry no
identity or device identifier; only the app version and compatibility information are sent. As
with any request, our server writes your IP address, the time of the request, the requested address
and the browser/app information (user agent) to its access logs.

### Installation ID

If you turn on answer analytics or use Challenge, our server generates a random ID for this
installation and an access token valid for 90 days. This ID is not linked to your name, e-mail
address, phone number or advertising ID; it is only used to recognize requests from the same
installation.

### Answer analytics (optional, off by default)

If you turn on **Settings → Answer analytics**, the following is sent: the category and mode you
play, the questions, the option you chose, the result (passed/failed and why), the elapsed time, a
limited interaction summary (press, release and tap counts, hold duration), the session score, the
app version, language and platform (iOS/Android). This data is used to improve question difficulty
and game balance. When you turn it off, collection stops and unsent events are cleared from the
device.

### Challenge (in versions where it is enabled, with separate consent)

To use Challenge you accept its rules separately. Our server then stores: a nickname **generated by
the system** (you do not type any text; you can only ask for a new name), match results, scores and
input evidence used to verify the score. Your nickname and score are shown to your rival and to
other players on the leaderboard. For live matches the app opens an encrypted real-time connection
to our server.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

In versions that show ads, ads are served by Google's advertising service **Google AdMob**. No ads
are shown during gameplay. To serve ads, Google may process: your advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, the
advertising ID on Android), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad impression and
click information, and diagnostic data. This data is sent directly to Google by Google's software
inside the app and is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy);
see also [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

- **EU, United Kingdom and Switzerland:** we ask for your consent to personalized ads with Google's
  consent form. If you do not consent, only non-personalized ads are shown.
- **US states:** you can exercise the rights granted by state privacy laws from the ad privacy
  options.
- **Türkiye:** on devices in Türkiye only non-personalized ads are requested.
- **iOS:** Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission is requested before the advertising
  ID is accessed. If you do not allow it, your advertising ID is not used. You can change this at
  any time in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads**
  (the menu name may vary by device).
- Ads are limited to content suitable for ages 13 and up; sensitive categories such as dating and
  gambling are blocked.

You can change your consent in the app with **Settings → Ad privacy options** (this option appears
when it is required in your region).

## Permissions and device features

- Hoppala does not ask for access to the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.
- **Vibration:** used for haptic feedback; it can be turned off in Settings.
- **Internet:** used to download content and (when enabled) for answer analytics, Challenge and
  ads.
- **Tracking permission (iOS only, in versions with ads):** see the Ads section above.

## Purposes and legal bases

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR) we process your data for the following purposes and on the following legal bases:

- **Delivering content, keeping the service secure and preventing abuse** (access logs,
  installation ID): necessity for providing the service and our legitimate interest; for access
  logs, also the legal obligation under Turkish Law No. 5651.
- **Answer analytics and Challenge:** your consent. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings;
  this does not affect processing before the withdrawal.
- **Personalized ads:** your consent where required; for non-personalized ads, our legitimate
  interest (offering the app free of charge).

## Retention periods

- **Server access logs** (IP address, time, requested address, user agent): 2 years.
- **Application error logs** (no IP address or request content is written): 2 years.
- **Answer analytics:** raw events 30 days, records linkable to the installation 90 days; aggregate
  statistics without identifiers 12 months.
- **Challenge profile, matches and scores:** 90 days.
- **Installation ID:** until you delete it; the access token is valid for 90 days.
- **Deletion record:** to confirm that a deletion request was completed and to prevent deleted data
  from coming back from a backup, a deletion record for the deleted installation is kept for 90
  days.
- **Backups:** deleted data may remain in server backups for up to 35 more days.

At the end of the period data is deleted or anonymized. Google's retention periods apply to
Google's advertising data.

## Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it for advertising ourselves. Data is processed
only by:

- **Hosting:** our server runs on Microsoft Azure; Microsoft only hosts the data on our behalf.
- **Google (AdMob):** in versions that show ads, the data described in the Ads section above.
- **Apple and Google:** they distribute the app through the App Store and Google Play; data these
  stores collect (e.g. download and crash statistics) is subject to their policies.
- **Authorities:** only when a legal obligation requires it.

The servers of the hosting and advertising providers may be located outside Türkiye. International
transfers rely on the safeguards provided by KVKK and the GDPR and, where required, on your
explicit consent.

## Data deletion

- **Data on our server:** **Settings → Delete my server data** requests deletion of your answer
  analytics data, Challenge profile, match participation and leaderboard scores on our server and
  invalidates the installation ID. You can follow the status on the same screen. Progress and
  settings on the device are kept.
- **Data on your device:** delete your progress with **Settings → Reset progress**, or uninstall
  the app.
- **By e-mail:** if you cannot access the app, write to the address in the **Contact** section
  below. Because we do not know your name, we may ask only for the information needed to find your
  data (e.g. your Challenge nickname).

Hoppala has no accounts, so there is no separate account to delete.

## Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK and the GDPR you have the right to learn whether your data is processed,
to request information, to rectification, to erasure, to object to processing, to withdraw consent
and (where applicable) to data portability. Send your requests to the address in the **Contact**
section below; we reply within 30 days at the latest. You can also lodge a complaint with the
Turkish Personal Data Protection Board or the data protection authority in your country.

## Children

Hoppala is designed for ages 13 and up; it is not directed at children under 13 and does not
knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has shared data, write to us and
we will delete it.

## Security

All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted with HTTPS. The access token is kept
in the device's secure storage; only authorized people can access the data on our server. No
method of storage or transmission is perfect. To report a vulnerability, see the
[Security page](/en/projects/hoppala/security/).

## This website

This page is published on our personal website hosted on GitHub Pages. The site uses no cookies,
analytics or tracking; only your light / dark theme choice is remembered in your browser's local
storage and is never sent anywhere. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to
serve the site; see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes (e.g. when a new feature is enabled), we update this
page and its effective date before the change is released.
