For questions, bug reports, question suggestions and feedback, write to the e-mail address in the
**Contact** section below. We usually reply within a few days.

When reporting a bug, please include:

- the app version (shown on the app's App Store or Google Play page or in your device's app
  settings);
- your device model and operating system version;
- which category and mode you were in and the steps to reproduce the problem; a screenshot if
  possible.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to create an account?

No. Hoppala has no account or sign-up, and we never ask for your e-mail address. You can play as
soon as you open the app.

### Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. The built-in categories can be played offline. New levels and content updates are downloaded
when you are online.

### Does my progress move to my new phone?

No. Your progress is not tied to an account, so it is stored only on your device. If you uninstall
the app or change devices, your progress starts from scratch.

### How do I reset my progress?

Tap **Settings → Reset progress**. This cannot be undone.

### How do I change the language, sound or haptics?

In **Settings** you can choose Turkish or English and turn **Sound effects** and **Haptics** on or
off. On first launch the language follows your device language.

### An answer looks wrong.

Some instructions are misleading on purpose; that is the core idea of Hoppala. If you still think
an answer is really wrong, send us the category and the question text (with a screenshot if
possible).

### How do I delete my data on your server?

Tap **Settings → Delete my server data**. For details and other options, see the "Data deletion"
section of the [Privacy Policy](/en/projects/hoppala/privacy/#data-deletion).

### How do I change my ad preferences?

In versions that show ads, **Settings → Ad privacy options** appears when it is required in your
region. On iOS you can change the tracking permission in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security →
Tracking**. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](/en/projects/hoppala/privacy/).

### I found a security vulnerability.

Please follow the [vulnerability reporting process](/en/projects/hoppala/security/).
